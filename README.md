A curated list of awesome team chat platforms, tools, and resources for modern remote and hybrid teams. Find the best communication hub for your company! This repo is part of the [Awesome-Awesome-Awesome](https://github.com/ishandutta2007/Awesome-Awesome-Awesome) network.

## 💼 SaaS Products

| Product | Description | Company Size (Valuation/Revenue) | Pricing | Free Tier Limit |
|---------|-------------|----------------------------------|---------|-----------------|
| Microsoft Teams | Unified communication platform | ~$3 Trillion (Microsoft Market Cap) | Starts at $4.00/user/month | Up to 100 users, 60 min meetings |
| Google Chat | Google's communication software | ~$2 Trillion (Alphabet Market Cap) | Included in Workspace ($6.00/user/month) | Available with free Google accounts |
| Zoom Team Chat | Team chat built into Zoom | ~$20 Billion (Zoom Market Cap) | Included in Zoom Pro ($14.99/month) | Basic version is free |
| Slack | Popular team communication tool | ~$27.7 Billion (Acquired by Salesforce) | Starts at $7.25/user/month | 90 days of message history |
| Discord | Voice, video and text service | ~$15 Billion (Valuation) | Free, Nitro starts at $2.99/month | Unlimited users, limited upload sizes |
| Mattermost | Open-source enterprise alternative | ~$100 Million+ (Estimated) | Starts at $10.00/user/month | Open-source self-hosted is free |
